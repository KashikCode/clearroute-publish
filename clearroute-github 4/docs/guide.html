PROJECT NOTES

How this desk works.

A supervised text-classification baseline, with the review process kept visible.

From narrative to destination

Clearroute uses the words in a complaint to predict a financial-product category. TF-IDF gives words and pairs of adjacent words numerical weights. A linear support-vector classifier scores each category. The highest score becomes the suggested category; an editable rule maps that category to a team.

You review the suggestion before confirming an assignment. Confirmation records a local destination. It does not contact the team, send email, or create a ticket in another system.

The training experiment

The included model was trained on 9,307 unique usable narratives from the CFPB July 2026 archive. Exact normalized duplicate narratives were removed before a stratified split into 6,514 training, 1,396 validation, and 1,397 test examples. The vocabulary and IDF were fit only on training text.

The experiment compares unigram SVM, unigram–bigram SVM, and class-balanced unigram–bigram SVM against a majority-class baseline. Model selection uses validation macro-F1. The untouched test set is used for the reported evaluation. Inspect the actual results.

Why show recall?

Accuracy counts all correct predictions together. Macro-F1 gives each product category equal weight. Recall asks how many complaints belonging to one category the model correctly identifies. Looking at all three reveals weaknesses that an overall accuracy number can obscure.

Recall is measured for product labels, not demographic groups. These results do not establish fairness across customer populations. A low-recall warning is based on the predicted category, so it cannot identify every complaint that belongs to a weak category but is predicted as a different one.

What stays on your device

Classification runs in JavaScript inside your browser. The app does not transmit complaint text to a classification service. Queue records and routing settings use session storage in the current browser tab. They are not shared with other users or devices. Export your queue before ending the session if you need a copy.

Use it within its scope

This is an English-language financial-product classifier. It has not been validated for restaurant, retail, or other general-business complaints. SVM scores are not probabilities, and the review thresholds are heuristics. A random test split also does not establish how well the model will perform on future complaints or a different customer population.

The public narratives are selected, unverified consumer accounts. Near-duplicate templates may remain after exact deduplication. The July 2026 archive is a fixed snapshot, not a live data feed.
